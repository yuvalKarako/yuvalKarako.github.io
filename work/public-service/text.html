# Digital transformation in the IDF

Owning digital transformation end to end.

I head digital transformation in the IDF Education & Youth Corps, with end-to-end ownership of its software systems and digitization projects: from architecture and specification, through development and deployment, to the teams that build and maintain them.

Context: Head of Digital Transformation · IDF Education & Youth Corps

Status: CURRENT ROLE

## The role

The work runs the full length of a product. I design system architecture and data, write the specifications, build and mentor development teams, and bring AI-assisted development into the way they work. What gets built, and in what order, is set against the Corps’ goals together with the Head of Digital.

## What it takes

A system gets used only when the people who define the need, the people who build it and the people who will use it agree on what it is for. Much of my work is translation between those three groups.

## Before this

During my regular service I was a product manager in the IDF (2020–22): mapping how a large organization actually works, defining the product, and presenting roadmaps and MVPs to decision-makers. That is where I learned how large organizations adopt technology. Before the army, I co-founded a small studio that published two mobile games.
