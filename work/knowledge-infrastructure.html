Knowledge as infrastructure

Notes and documents become more useful when their relationships, sources, and context remain accessible. I develop and teach methods for building personal knowledge bases that support sustained collaboration with AI.

A connected workflow

This work brings together Obsidian, structured knowledge, version control, and agentic tools. The emphasis is on preserving a usable connection between original sources, accumulated understanding, and the work being produced.

A compounding asset

A good knowledge base should become more valuable as it is used. Each project can contribute context and structure that make the next project easier to begin and better informed.
