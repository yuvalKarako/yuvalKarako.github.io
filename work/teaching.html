The program

HIGH AI trains senior academics to work with agentic AI tools. The participants are full professors and heads of departments and institutes: people with decades of expertise and very little time. I went through the program myself before joining its teaching staff.

What I teach

A 90-minute unit on building a personal knowledge base, with live demonstrations and hands-on work in Claude, Obsidian and Git. The starting point is each participant’s own papers, data and notes, not a generic example. Everyone leaves with a first concrete step for their own research.

Why it matters

Models change every few months; a researcher’s accumulated knowledge does not. Organized well, it gives AI the context to be useful, and gives the researcher a way to check what the AI produced. I run my own research this way, and I am writing the method up as a paper.

What came out of it

The classroom has also become a place where research starts: the Convergence grant began with a conversation in the program.
