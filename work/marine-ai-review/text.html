# AI for marine monitoring

Understand the field. Find the missing questions.

Examining how AI is used in marine ecological monitoring, and the gap between visual detection and ecological interpretation.

Context: Marine ecology · Computer vision · Evidence synthesis

Status: RESEARCH IN PROGRESS

## Mapping the research landscape

This research direction examines AI methods for marine ecological monitoring, with particular attention to the relationship between visual perception and ecological understanding.

## What evidence can support

A useful synthesis must distinguish the task a model performs from the ecological conclusions its outputs can support. Detection, measurement, interpretation, and causal explanation ask different things of a system.

## Connection to the broader work

Understanding those distinctions helps frame the questions and evaluation criteria for my own research on ecological intelligence.
