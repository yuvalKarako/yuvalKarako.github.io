# Teaching agentic AI

Make accumulated knowledge usable.

Teaching researchers and senior academics to build personal knowledge bases and put agentic AI to work — connecting deep domain expertise with new ways of thinking and building.

Context: Teaching faculty · HIGH AI · Bar-Ilan University

Status: TEACHING & PRACTICE

## Expertise meets a new toolset

As part of HIGH AI’s teaching faculty, I teach agentic workflows, Second Brains, and personal knowledge bases. My work focuses on helping researchers and senior academics put AI to use in their own fields.

## Start with what people know

The starting point is the learner’s own research, documents, and accumulated knowledge. Organizing that material gives AI a useful context and gives the human a way to evaluate and improve the result.

## From a session to a practice

The aim is a repeatable way of working: capture knowledge, organize it, retrieve what matters, and use agents to help turn it into work that can be checked and refined.
