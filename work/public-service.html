The role

The Corps works with a small digital team and little technical infrastructure. My job is to bring current technology, AI included, to problems that would otherwise need more people or more money: defining the product, writing the specification, and showing commanders a working demonstration they can judge for themselves.

What it takes

A system gets used only when the people who define the need, the people who build it and the people who will use it agree on what it is for. Much of my work is translation between those three groups.

Before this

During my regular service I was a product manager in the IDF (2020–22): mapping how a large organization actually works, defining the product, and presenting roadmaps and MVPs to decision-makers. That is where I learned how large organizations adopt technology. Before the army, I co-founded a small studio that published two mobile games.
